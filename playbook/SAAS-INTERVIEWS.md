# PlateLog Problem Interviews

**Goal:** find out whether HVAC owners lose money on unregistered warranties and unmanaged maintenance plans, and whether they'd pay to fix it. Don't pitch until the last two minutes. You're learning, not selling.

**Who:** owners or office managers of 1–10 truck residential HVAC shops. Run 10 interviews before deciding anything. Start with your website and text-back prospects and clients. Every call you're already making is a chance.

**Length:** 10 minutes. Ask for exactly that and stick to it.

---

## Asking for the interview

**On a call you're already on (website or text-back pitch):**
> Before I let you go, can I ask you three quick questions about how you track installs? I'm looking into building a tool for HVAC shops and I'd rather ask owners than guess.

**Cold call or text:**
> Hi <name>, <you> here. I work with HVAC shops around KC. I'm researching how small shops handle warranty registration and maintenance plans. Can I buy you a coffee or get 10 minutes on the phone this week? I'm not selling anything on this call.

**At a supply house counter:**
> Who here does the most installs? I want to ask a couple of owners how they keep track of warranty paperwork.

---

## The script

### 1. Warm-up (1 min)
1. How many trucks and techs do you run?
2. Roughly how many system installs or replacements a month?
3. What do you run the business on: a field-service app (Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan), QuickBooks, paper, or a mix?

### 2. Install records (2 min)
4. When a tech finishes an install, what happens to the model and serial numbers? Walk me through the last one.
5. Who types it in, and where does it end up?
6. If a customer calls in 3 years, how do you find what's in their house?

**Listen for:** photos sitting in a tech's phone, handwritten job tickets, "the office types it in eventually," "we just look at the unit."

### 3. Warranty registration (3 min): the core pain
7. Who registers the equipment with the manufacturer, and when?
8. How do you know it actually got done?
9. **Tell me about the last time a unit wasn't registered.** What happened?
10. Have you ever covered parts or labor yourself because coverage had lapsed or registration was missed? Roughly what did it cost?
11. How many installs a year do you think slip through without registration?

**Listen for a dollar amount.** "We ate a $1,400 compressor last summer" is the strongest signal you can get. If they register 100% reliably and have never lost money, note it. That's a no for this feature, and that's fine.

### 4. Maintenance plans (2 min)
12. Do you sell maintenance agreements or tune-up plans? How many members?
13. How do members get reminded about their spring and fall tune-ups?
14. What happens when someone's plan comes up for renewal?
15. If you had to guess, how many tune-ups or renewals fall through the cracks each year?

**Listen for:** "we're supposed to call them," a spreadsheet, a wall calendar, or "we tried but stopped."

### 5. What they've tried (1 min)
16. Have you tried any software or system for this? What happened?
17. If you could wave a wand and fix one thing about installs or warranty paperwork, what would it be?

### 6. Pitch test (1 min): only now
> Here's what I'm thinking about building. Your tech snaps a photo of the data plate after an install. It reads the model, serial and manufacture date, saves the install record, tracks the warranty registration deadline, and texts your maintenance customers when their tune-up is due. Can I show you a 30-second demo?

Open the PlateLog demo on your phone and scan a data plate, or use the sample plate. Then ask:

18. Would your techs actually take that photo? What would stop them?
19. **If this existed today at $99 a month, would you use it?**
20. **I'm taking 5 founding shops at $49 a month, locked in for life. It starts when it launches, and you don't pay until it does. Want one of the spots?**

If they say yes, get their name, email and cell. That's a presale. A "maybe" counts as a no.

### Close
> Thanks, this helped a lot. Can I text you when there's something to try? And who's one other owner I should talk to?

## Deciding after 10 interviews

| Signal | Build it | Change direction | Drop it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shops that lost money on registration | 4+ | 2–3 | 0–1 |
| Founding spots claimed | 3+ | 1–2 | 0 |
| "Techs won't take the photo" | Rare | Common (look at office-side entry instead) | Universal |

- **If warranty pain is weak but the maintenance-plan pain is strong:** pivot the product to the maintenance-plan manager. The interviews already cover both.
- **Don't count compliments.** "Cool idea" is worth nothing. Count dollars lost and spots claimed.

## Rules for the interviewer
- Ask about **the last time it happened**, not "would you ever." Past behavior is fact. Future predictions are polite fiction.
- Let silence sit. The best answers come after a pause.
- Never argue with an answer.
- Write down their exact words. They become your landing-page copy.
