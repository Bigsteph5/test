What Kansas City homeowners ask us most before they book.

Call (816) 436-8475 and we'll give you the soonest arrival window we have.

Should I repair or replace my system?

A quick rule: multiply the system's age by the repair cost. If the result is over $5,000, replacement usually makes more sense. Systems over 15 years old, or AC units that still use R-22 refrigerant, are often better replaced. We give you both prices so you can decide.

Do heat pumps work in a Kansas City winter?

Yes. Cold-climate heat pumps keep most of their heating capacity well below freezing. Many Kansas City homes use dual-fuel: the heat pump handles the bulk of the winter, and a gas furnace takes over on the coldest nights, so you always pay the cheaper rate.

Do you pull permits and handle rebates?

Yes. We pull the permits your city requires and help with any utility rebate paperwork your new equipment qualifies for.
